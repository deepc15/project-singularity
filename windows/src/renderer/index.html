This is the receiving screen

Open Singular Cast on your phone, pick this PC, then swipe an app toward the edge you configured. It will appear here as a tile, sized to fit.

  • Mouse and keyboard work directly on each tile.
  • Right-click is Android Back; the scroll wheel scrolls the app.
  • Bring back to phone returns the app to the device.
  • Resize a tile and the app re-lays itself out at the new size.

Both devices must be on the same network. If discovery is blocked, type this PC's IP into Singular Cast manually.
